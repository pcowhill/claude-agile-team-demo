**A flake was measured and fixed instead of counted out.** #926,
`export-wav.spec.ts:135`, had closed by count and recurred. #973 measured
its cause: Chromium's ScriptProcessor dropped a 128-frame quantum when its
main-thread side was late, and 13 of 24 exported WAVs under load had a
splice. With an AudioWorklet tap, 0 of 24 did. That is the route #968
proposes for every flake that recurs after closing by count.

**A review caught a race that CI had passed.** #975's first review ran
the new full-screen spec ten times locally. It failed **8 of 10**: Space
landed on View ▾'s trigger before the stage had taken the focus. CI had
passed it once in both browsers, so it would have reached `main` as a
flake. The fix waits for the focus, and 40 of 40 runs then passed.

**#870 closed, and #871 split cleanly.** #979 shipped #870's last step.
#871 was split into three steps on 2026-11-26 at 02:35. Step 1 (#981)
merged 65 min after opening, and step 2a (#982) 39 min after
opening. Step 1's claim comment named its convergence points with #979,
then open, and the conflict there was resolved in one merge commit
(`cc8a0b4`).

**#779's dispatched-run route set the mechanism before the code.** #977
built the channel-offset filter on numbers from dispatched run
45720323678, which measured the SVG filter in CSS and in a canvas in both
browsers first. Chromatic aberration and Glitch then adopted the kind
with no renderer code of their own.

## Went poorly

**Firefox still finds what the container cannot.** #979's first push
failed CI's Firefox job. Its fringe bound was symmetric, but Firefox
truncates a fractional `feOffset` where Chromium rounds it (#980). The
reviewer widened the bound in `18e1f69`, at the cost of a review round
and a second CI run: open to merge was 77 min. The previous retrospective
recorded three PRs held the same way (#937, #942, #952). #977's
measurement had read both browsers, but at whole-pixel shifts, so the
truncation did not show until a fractional one was drawn. The route
works only for the cases a measurement thinks to include.

**#967 recurred again after closing by count.** `trim-window.spec.ts:65`
in Firefox closed at five clean runs at 21:37 and failed again at 02:34,
in #979's run 45743375798. That is its sixth sighting across #741 and
#967. It closed by count a second time at 04:41, this session. #968, the previous retrospective's proposal to measure such a flake
rather than count it out again, has had no PR yet. This period added one
more data point for it, and #926's fix above is the other half of the
argument.

**Two measurements of one cost disagree, and nobody has reconciled them.**
#978 measured VHS's colour offset at 24.7–25.3 fps in a 1203 px preview,
and a single `feOffset` inside VHS at 25.8 fps. #981 and #982 then
measured the same `url()` filter alone, as Chromatic aberration and
Glitch, at 29–30 fps at the same size. #978's second comment names the
measurement that would settle it: VHS with each of its parts removed in
turn.
