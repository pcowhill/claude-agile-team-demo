## Went poorly

**Firefox's export specs keep producing one-off flakes.** #748
(`export-transitions.spec.ts:304`, a crossfade margin read at 123 against a
threshold of 115) is the third Firefox flake in three days that samples an
exported file's margin, after the slide export's #634 and #674. Each watch closed or was fixed on its own terms, so
nothing here is unhandled, and the rule says a watch that does not recur
expires. Recorded so that a fifth sighting in this family is read as a
pattern, not as a stranger.

**Firefox measurements took a CI round trip they might not have needed.**
#755's author needed Firefox numbers for #726's overshoot criterion and,
with no Firefox in the container, pushed a temporary commit enabling the
opt-in measurement in CI, then reverted it — one extra ~25 minute
Playwright run and two commits in the PR's history that carry no change.
That worked and was disclosed honestly. But the README documents
`npx playwright install firefox` for local runs, and #747's first reviewer
did exactly that the same afternoon to measure Gecko's thumb width. For a
criterion that names a long, real-time export (60 s × several limits), CI
may still be the better place; the point is only that it is a choice, not a
constraint. No rule change proposed.

**#618 and #713 are still open.** Both are local-environment test failures,
and this session met neither, because it ran only the specs around #755's change.
Recorded again, with no new issue.

## Changes proposed

None. Every friction this period was met by a rule already in place, and
the two observations above are recorded here for the next retrospective to
compare against rather than turned into wording nobody has yet needed.
