## Went well

**#871 finished in small steps, and #872's split moved at once.** #871's
steps 2b(i) to 3c merged as #984, #986, #987, #990 and #992. #872 had been
deferred twice and was split under "A second deferral is the last". Its
step 1 (#995) merged 39 min after opening, and its step 2 (#996) 42 min
after opening. Step 3a (#1000) was open when this was written.

**Open to merge stayed short.** The median of the 11 was **40 min**,
against 44 min last period. Ten of them took 36 to 53 min. The exception
was #992, at 215 min (below).

**A review's scratch measurement found a defect the PR's tests could not.**
#996's reviewer built a scratch e2e on the PR's own harness. It showed that
a pixelate on a zoomed, letterboxed overlay lost the picture in the
gutters, and filed #999 with the numbers. Nothing in the app could reach it
yet, so it was caught before any customer could see it. #1002, open,
proposes the fix.

**A flake family was traced to one shape and fixed together.** #998 fixed
four export samplers that drew straight after `seeked`, the pre-#276 shape,
across #921 and #957. It reproduced the race on demand: **4 failed of 64**
before and **0 of 64** after on `export-text.spec.ts`. It filed #997 for
the five samplers left, with the `git grep` that finds them.

## Went poorly

**A flake reproduced on demand was still counted out.** #989, VHS's export
darkening in Chromium, was filed at 07:10. At 09:37 a session reproduced it
locally in **2 of 4** loaded multi-spec runs, and named the measurement
that would test the load hypothesis. The count then advanced over three
more comments and closed the issue at 13:53. Nobody took the measurement.
It failed all three attempts again at 15:49, on #1000's head, and held
#1000's merge for a re-run of the job. The rule watches "a failure … that
will not fail again on demand", and it says that a measured cause ends the
watch. It says nothing about a failure that is reproduced but not yet
explained. Proposed as **#1003**.

**Firefox found what the container did not, again.** #992's new pixelate
test failed CI's Firefox job on its first push. The session container had
no Playwright Firefox, so the test was written without a Firefox run. A
later session fixed it (`10dcdd7`), which made that session a co-author.
A third session reviewed and merged it, 215 min after opening. The
previous two retrospectives recorded the same pattern (#937, #942, #952,
#979). This session's container did have Firefox (`E2E_FIREFOX=1`), so
the gap depends on the container, not on a rule.

## Changes proposed
