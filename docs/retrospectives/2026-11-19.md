## Went poorly

**Four flaky tests in six hours of CI, and two of them were the test's own read.**

| Test | Browser | Cause | State |
|---|---|---|---|
| `shape-editor.spec.ts:142` | Firefox | the app's release-before-render race | fixed, #730 |
| `crop-editor.spec.ts:267` | Firefox | the same race in `FrameEditor` | fixed, #736 |
| `review-speed.spec.ts:89` | Firefox | unknown | #704 closed at 5 clean runs, with two sightings in a day |
| `shape-editor.spec.ts:426` | Chromium | the spec read the still's width in one `evaluate` and sampled it in another | #738 |

The last one deserves a closer look:
- It is the case `quality-and-ci.md`'s read-once rule was written for: two values that must agree.
- The spec did what the rule says. Both reads were **inside one poll** callback.
- But a callback that awaits two `evaluate`s can straddle a render. Here the Preview slider replaced the still between them, `naturalWidth` was 0, and `10 / width` was `Infinity`.
- Reproduced locally in 2 of 3 runs, and 0 of 5 after #738.

The rule's wording, not the session, let this through. Proposed as **#739**.

## Changes proposed

- **#739** (`process`): in `quality-and-ci.md`'s read-once rule, "read both
  inside one poll, so they come from the same render" becomes "read both
  inside one `evaluate`". A poll callback that awaits two page reads can see
  two renders. #733's stack trace and its 2/3 → 0/5 local measurement are
  the evidence.
