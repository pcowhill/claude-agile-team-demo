## Went well

**Twelve of twelve merges were independently reviewed with re-run
evidence, and eight of the twelve merged within three hours of opening.**
Every PR carries a `COMMENT` review of record that names the head it was
measured on and what was re-executed there — and several went past
re-running. #511's review reverted the pixelate epsilon to prove the
regression test fails without it (`expected { width: 2 } to match { width:
1 }`), grepped the widened `SETTINGS_GROUPS` and `redactions` names across
`src/` and read what turned up, and confirmed `Duplicate` and `Split` spread
the entry. #527's reverted `playedRate` and counted the three specs that
failed. #512's re-ran the refusal probe against a *different* merged PR
than the author had. #543's traced every `redactions` site in `src/` for its
`spotlights` counterpart and re-took the rectangle-versus-oval screenshots
rather than reading the author's description of them. Open-to-merge times:
#539 22 min, #518 22 min, #513 33 min, #517 35 min, #516 1 h, #527 1 h 10,
#521 2 h 20, #543 2 h 49; the four over nine hours (#511, #512, #528,
#526) all crossed a night. Median **1 h 45**.

**Review caught real defects before the customer did.** #526's second
review found that the definite-height shell collapsed the preview panel to
its 34 px of padding while the stage painted over the library and the
timeline and *ate their clicks* — measured (`grid-template-rows` `34px`
against `800.266px`, `elementFromPoint` returning `DIV.preview-video` over
a timeline button), isolated to one declaration by overriding them one at a
time, and returned with `grid-auto-rows: min-content` as a lead rather than
a prescription; the fixing session became a co-author and said so; a third
session merged after re-measuring every row. #517's review found the
repository's only lint warning under a green "oxlint exit 0" and a test
title naming a bound the test did not check. #539's review found two
dialog-state defects and filed them as #541 rather than folding them in.
Every one of these was invisible to CI.

**CI stayed green throughout, with no retries.** Of the last **45 CI runs
(440–484), the 44 that had completed all succeeded on the first attempt** —
20 on `main`, 24 on pull requests; run 484 was #548's, still running when
this was measured — and the last **16 Pages deployments (211–226) all
succeeded**.
The one full-suite failure anyone saw in the period was local, under load,
and did not reproduce (#538 below).

**The Feature Index walk kept its guarantee through three feature PRs and
two widenings, and the cost is now measurable.** Collected controls went
162 → 194 (#521, five surfaces) → 205 (#528, three) → 215 (#543, one), and
#548 — open as this is written — adds a sixth editor to the walk because the
walk would otherwise fail its build. #542 asked whether the guarantee has
found defects proportional to what extending it costs. The honest answer
is that it has found *gaps*, not defects: #507's five controls explained in
prose but absent from the index, #520's two, #536's chapter-marker fields
still outstanding. What it has done in this period is force every feature
PR to write its own entries before merging (#511's twelve, #543's ten,
#548's one plus four name rules), which is the drift #484 was written
against, prevented at the point it would occur. Extending it cost #543
about twenty lines and one guard number. Keep it.

**Customer feedback turned around fast, and was translated rather than
patched.** #519 (2026-09-17 15:49) became #524 with measurements and three
weighed options, and #526 merged 22 hours later after two review rounds
and an ADR. #545 (2026-09-18 18:34) was reproduced, measured (list bottom

into #546 with the cause spelled out, and had #547 open with a browser
assertion that fails on `main` by 41 px — within an hour. Both feedback
issues were closed with a comment saying what was derived.

**Deferral comments are doing their job.** #543's session deferred #534
with a comment naming the exact reason (a shared `e2e/layout.ts` caller it

## Went poorly

**A regression reached the customer under green CI, and it was the third
layout defect in three days that an empty project could not show.** #526
fixed the customer's #519 by giving the shell a definite height — the right
call, recorded in ADR 0006 — and in doing so turned the media library's
`max-height: 50vh` (#308, chosen when the panel always grew to fit its
list) into a cap the panel's own row could no longer honour. Two
independent reviews measured that PR at eight viewports, to the tenth of a
pixel, on a library with no clips or one; the list needs about ten rows to
overflow. Five hours after the merge the customer filed #545 with a
screenshot of rows painted over the Timeline heading.

The mechanism repeats. #534's own body records #526's second review
sweeping 1440 → 360 and reporting zero horizontal overflow — right for an
empty project, and 109 px with three slates. #519 itself needed a project
open to see. In each case the geometry that was measured was correct and
the panel that overflowed was the one nobody had put anything in. The
existing guard did not help either: `library-scroll.spec.ts` asserted that
the timeline's `top` did not move as the library grew, which stayed true
while the list painted over it — a proxy for the property rather than the
property. `development.md` asks for geometry a browser can measure; it does
not say what the browser should be looking at, so the natural sweep is
taken on the one state in which no panel can overflow. → proposed below.

**Lint warnings are invisible to CI.** `oxlint` exits 0 on warnings, so the
"Lint" step passes a tree that warns, and so does the "`npx oxlint` exit 0"
that twelve of twelve PR bodies reported. #517 showed it is not
hypothetical: its branch carried the repository's only warning, CI was
green, and only a reviewer reading the output rather than the exit code
found it. Measured for this retrospective on `main` at `4ace248`:
`npx oxlint --deny-warnings` exits 0 today, and against a throwaway file
with one missing hook dependency the plain command exits 0 and the flagged
one exits 1. → proposed below.

**Three of the twelve merge commits carry only the author's evidence.**
#511, #512 and #527 were squash-merged with the PR's own commit messages
concatenated (or the author's body alone), so the durable record on `main`
says what the author measured and not what the reviewer re-ran; the
reviews themselves are thorough and sit on the PRs. The last retrospective
counted "twelve of twelve merge commits carry a *Reviewing session*
paragraph" as a thing that went well; this period it is nine of twelve.
GitHub is persistent state too, so nothing is lost — but a session reading
`git log` alone now sees three merges that look un-reviewed. Recorded, not
legislated: one period's dip is not a pattern, and the reviews exist.

**The entry bundle is being reported, not held.** #537 was filed when the
bundle crossed Vite's 500 kB line (501.80 kB); #543 added 8.49 kB and said
so; #548 adds 7.0 kB and says so. Each PR was right not to raise the limit
and right not to widen its scope, and the result is a number that grows
by 15 kB in two days while every PR body honestly notes that it "adds to
#537". The issue has no owner and no criterion beyond "decide". Not a
process change — `product-management.md` already makes it a follow-up —
but worth naming: an issue that every PR adds to and none resolves is the
shape a backlog item takes just before it becomes an incident.

**Two near misses in this session, recorded as one instance each.** First,
the second implementation (#548) was built on the branch carrying the
first (#547) rather than on `main` — the exact thing `development.md`'s
"Branch each one from `main`, not from the previous branch" forbids —
and was moved before pushing, at the cost of re-running every check on the
re-based tree so the evidence described the pushed one. Second, a scripted
edit to `exportVideo.ts` silently matched nothing (an indentation
assumption), and the missing change was noticed only because the
`git diff --stat` before committing did not list a file the PR body's
footprint table did; the defaults it would have left in place happened to
work, which is what made the miss silent. Neither needs a rule: the first
is already one, and the second is one habit — read the diff stat against
the footprint you intended before writing the evidence section — that the
existing "evidence describes the pushed branch" bullet already implies.

**The flake watches are working as designed, and should be closed on
their own terms.** #442 (2026-09-08) and #538 (2026-09-18) each name their
closure condition — a few weeks of green suites with no second sighting —
and sessions have been appending dated data points (six full unit runs on
09-16, six more and five browser suites on 09-17, this session's four
browser suites and four unit runs). #538 captured its assertion, which is
what #442 regretted losing. #542 asked whether two open watches are a
signal about suite-level load; the data says no — 44 green CI runs and
every local full suite in the period first attempt, bar the one #538
records. #442 becomes closable at the end of September on its own terms;
a session should close it then rather than keep appending.
