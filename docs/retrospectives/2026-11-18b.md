## Went poorly

**The CI runner's apt step turned two ready PRs red.**
- In 2 of the last 30 runs, `install-deps` ran into its 4-minute cap. It normally takes 13–25 s.
- #682's retry then hit its own 8-minute cap.
- #671's retry succeeded, but the job then hit its 30-minute limit partway through Firefox. Its steps' caps plus the test time allow more than 30 minutes.
- Each one cost a manual re-run and 20–30 minutes.
- Filed as **#714**, with both runs' step timings.

**A design was approved with a shortcut the app already uses.**
- #703's trimming design gave I / O a new meaning in the main preview. The customer approved it as recommended.
- But I / O have been the export marks since #417.
- The PM pass caught it while deriving #710, moved #710 to Q / W, and offered the customer a switch.
- That was the right recovery, but the customer had approved a design that could not ship as written.
- `src/lib/shortcuts.ts` is the single table of app-level shortcuts, and one grep there returns the clash. Proposed as **#715**.

**Firefox-only layout failures are found only in CI.**
- #676's number boxes passed every local run and then failed Firefox CI twice (`field-units.spec.ts`: "needs 38px, the input shows 31px"). Firefox always draws a number spinner.
- Session containers have no Playwright Firefox, so this costs a full CI cycle each time.
- #712 named the same limit and leaned on CI for Firefox, which passed.
- No new issue: it is an environment fact, and the reviewers' handling was right. Recorded so the cost stays visible.

**#618 is open for a third period.** The 2026-11-17b retrospective called it
