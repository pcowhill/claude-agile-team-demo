## Went poorly

**#912's Firefox bound was set from three readings on fast runners.** They
read 4.0, 3.9 and 4.0 ms (#915 lists the runs). The fourth Firefox run
landed on a slow runner and turned `main` red. #917's table, 21 readings
over seven runs, is what a bound should have been set from. That is one
instance, so nothing is proposed. A bound on a CI timing reading that is
set from one or two runs is worth noticing in review.

**A widened kind kept a comment written for the narrow one (#933).** #933
adds a `grain` picture layer, the first that *lightens*. It kept #924's
comment on the export's outgoing-card path: "a darkening drawn over it
here equals the preview's darkened card at the same opacity".
- During a crossfade, white grain over the fading outgoing clip measured
  **42.3** in Save frame against **20.9** in the preview, as the mean
  brightness of a black still at the midpoint.
- The PR's tests covered grain with no transition. Its body records no
  widened-kinds grep.
- The review's grep found it. The fix is on the branch as `c382067`, and
  #933 now needs a further review.

item, a test exercising only the case where the defect cannot show:
- **last period: three instances**, #889, #904 and #908;
