## Went poorly

**The one changes-required round started from a deferral note.**
- #825's deferral said: *"The razor's own guard is `isSplittablePoint` … Apply's disabled rule ('on In, on Out, or outside the trimmed range') can read it instead of restating it."*
- That was right for the edges it named. But #837 made `isSplittablePoint` Apply's **whole** rule. The issue asked for Split at playhead's rules exactly, and that guard is `splitTargetAt`, which also refuses inside a transition.
- Review caught it, and the round cost 90 minutes and a third session.

A note naming a helper says where to start. The issue's criteria still say
what must hold. This is one instance, and review worked as designed, so no
rule change is proposed.

**PRs grew, and review slowed with them.**
- Six of the ten PRs added 1057–1584 lines (#815, #830, #837, #838, #839, #841). In the previous period the largest was #810, at 838.
- The median open-to-merge time was **136 min** (90 min without the three PRs that waited out the overnight gap), against 31 min last period.

The cause is visible in the PRs. Each Split or Cut feature brings:
- a new window;
- component tests through `<App/>`;
- e2e geometry at two widths, shown failing;
- guide passages and a property sweep in review.

Tests are roughly half of each diff. Nothing was rubber-stamped, and the
features are as small as the customer's request divides into. This is the
cost of the evidence rules, paid as intended. It is recorded so the next
retrospective can tell whether it keeps growing.

**The choice between two approved issues went unrecorded until afterwards.**
- This session claimed #828 over #834. Both are customer-approved, carry the same labels, and have no Project priority set.
- The reasons were that #828 finishes the earlier approval (#731, 12:03, against #829, 13:45) and had just been unblocked. They went into a comment on #834 only after the evidence for this retrospective pointed out the gap.

"Take the top issue first" (`operating-model.md`) asks for a comment when
the top issue is deferred for size. It does not ask for one when the
ranking itself is the judgment. One instance, fixed in place, so nothing is
proposed.

## Changes proposed

None. Each pattern above has one instance, and in each the existing
mechanism caught it: review, deferral comments, or this retrospective.
