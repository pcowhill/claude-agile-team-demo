# Retrospective 2026-09-03

Scope: the 31 PRs merged since the 2026-08-31 retrospective — #214 through
#291 (merged 2026-09-01 to 2026-09-03).

Trigger: 31 merged PRs against the "roughly every ten" cadence in
`docs/rules/process-improvement.md` — the fourth period running over cadence,
and by the widest margin yet. No failure pattern forced an earlier one.

Evidence reviewed: the merge commits on `main` since #214; every
main-branch workflow run in the period (CI runs 175–235 as numbered on
`main`: 31 CI runs and 31 Pages deploys, one pair per merge, each checked
for its conclusion); the #275 review thread end to end (changes-required
review, fix, merge-conflict resolution, second review); the flaky-test
issues #240 and #276 with their fixes #242 and #279 and the follow-up #284;
the customer feedback of the period (#264, #265, #280, #281, #282, #283,
#297) and its triage; the #285 design question and its approval; the #277
suggestion and its approval; the three-PR parallel handoff #289/#290/#291.

## Went well

**Main was green the entire period.** All 31 CI runs and all 31 deploys on
`main` concluded `success`. The suite grew from 837 to 1421 unit tests and
from 88 to 137 Playwright tests, all passing at period end.

**Independent review caught a real defect again, with executed evidence.**
The #275 review reproduced a preview/export parity bug (a blur backdrop
draw resetting the canvas filter under a color-adjusted incoming
transition layer) with a fake-context test that fails on the branch,
required changes, and the fix was pinned by a composer test. The
three-session chain (reviewer → fixer/co-author → second reviewer) held,
and the second reviewer's mechanical merge-conflict resolution was
declared as such and did not transfer authorship.

**Flaky tests were treated as bugs and root-caused, not silenced.** #240
turned out to be a product race (Discard dismissed before the IndexedDB
clear committed) and was fixed in the product (#242); #276 was a test race
(sampling a sought frame before it was presented) and was fixed once in a
shared helper (#279), with the last private copy removed in #284. No
assertion was weakened.

**Bounded parallelism worked.** One session opened three independent PRs
(#289, #290, #291) under the WIP cap; a later session reviewed and merged
all three without conflicts, and the two PRs that did conflict earlier
(#275 against #278/#279) were resolved mechanically in a documented step.

**Design-first customer questions are fast and cheap.** #280 asked for a
design before implementation; #285 proposed one with alternatives and a
recommendation; the customer approved within the hour; #292/#293 were
derived the same session. #277 (image overlays) went from suggestion to
approved, scoped issues (#294/#295) the same way.

## Went poorly

**Two visible layout defects shipped green and were found by the
customer.** The export-modal format note rendered beside the radios and
overflowed the dialog (#264/#265 → #268, fixed #270); the shortcut cheat
sheet wrapped a key combo mid-combo (#282 → #287, fixed #289). Both
features had honest component tests — in jsdom, which has no layout — and
neither PR reported looking at the rendered result. Nothing in the
Definition of Done asked for it.

**A feature interaction regressed a safety signal.** Session restore
(#194) reused the Open Project replacement path, which resets the
unsaved-changes baseline (#76) — correct for opening a file, wrong for
restoring never-saved work. The customer noticed the indicator vanish
(#283 → #288, fixed #290). #194's acceptance criteria covered restore
fidelity but not the invariants of the path it reused. Observation only:
one instance, and the fix is in; the lesson for issue authors is to list
the existing invariants a reused flow carries when writing criteria.

**The retrospective trigger keeps being missed.** 31 PRs between
retrospectives, after 10 (2026-08-31) that was already noted as double the
cadence. The trigger is written down but is not part of the orientation
routine, so it depends on a session remembering it.

**A test helper was duplicated across parallel PRs.** #275 (written
before #279 landed) carried its own copy of the decoded-frame sampler,
which needed a third PR (#284) to remove. Small cost, already paid; noted
so parallel authors prefer extracting shared e2e helpers early.

## Changes proposed

- #301 — UI-affecting PRs must carry rendered-layout evidence (a Playwright
  geometry assertion or an inspected screenshot), stated in "How it was
  tested"; jsdom alone is not evidence for layout.
- #302 — make the retrospective trigger an explicit orientation step
  (count merged PRs since the newest retrospective file; ten or more means
  one is due), with a "Retrospective due" `process` issue as the fallback
  when a session has higher-priority work.
