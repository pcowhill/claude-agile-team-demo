## Went poorly

**CI wall time set every merge's pace.** Leaving out #1039, the other 12
PRs took 36 to 71 min from open to merge, with a median of 43.4 min. The 71
was #1030's reviewer fix and its second CI run. #1039 waited 5.7 h for
the next `Go` run to start, and no session was running in that gap.
That median is almost exactly one CI run. On #1044's run, the Playwright
job's steps were:
- setup: 19 s;
- Chromium: 19 m 01 s;
- Firefox: **26 m 48 s, after Chromium rather than beside it**;
- total: 46 m 11 s.

Under `Go X`, subagents are serial, so each PR's review waits out that
time once. This session waited on two PRs, back to back. Proposed as
**#1047**: run the two browsers as parallel jobs, about 19 min less per
run. The issue lists what the change must carry. The one thing that
cites the job's name is `quality-and-ci.md`'s flake-watch rule.

**A visual defect class was fixed one kind at a time.** #1037 gave volume
changes a lane of their own because their bar hid a zoom's. The same
cover-up between zooms, speed segments and picture effects then waited
as #1038 until this session's #1046. That was not a mistake: #1037 was
right to stay in scope and file the rest. But the shape, bars of
different kinds sharing one lane, was visible from the first Trim
window marks (#709). There is no rule to change. A review that sees a
new kind of mark can ask where the old kinds sit.

## Changes proposed

- **#1047:** run Chromium and Firefox e2e as two parallel CI jobs, and
  update the flake-watch rule's job name with it.
